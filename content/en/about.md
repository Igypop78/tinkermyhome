Hi, I'm **Igy**. By day I do a completely different job, but in the evenings I tinker with the house we live in – four of us: me, my wife Katka, our sons Martin and Jakub, and Megie the dog. It started with one smart bulb. Today we have solar with a battery that trades on spot prices by itself, an electric car that charges when power is cheapest, and Home Assistant holding it all together.

## Why TinkerMyHome

Smart homes are usually shown as if from a catalogue: everything lights up, everything works, nothing costs money. We show what it looks like in an ordinary family. What it really costs, what pays off, what works for the people who didn't set it up – and where we got it wrong.

## Our rules

- **Real numbers.** Consumption, bills and prices come from our house. We don't estimate and we don't round in our favour.
- **Mistakes included.** We show mistakes as openly as successes. You often learn more from them.
- **No ads in disguise.** Nobody pays us. If an article or video ever has a link that earns us a commission, we'll say so clearly.
- **Only what we have at home.** We only write and film about things we bought and use.

## Who's who

## Videos

From 3 December, a new video from our house goes up on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@TinkerMyHome) every Thursday (in Slovak). Each video has an article here with the technical details and settings to copy.

## How this site is made

AI (Claude) helps us build the site, write the articles and edit the videos, based on my notes, configs and data from the house. I review and approve every article and video before it goes live. The voice in the videos is mine.
