Hi, I'm **Igy**. By day I do a completely different job, but in the evenings I tinker with my own house. It started with one smart bulb. Today I run Home Assistant with thousands of entities, solar with a battery that trades on spot prices by itself, my own ESP32 sensors and a homelab on Proxmox.

I write **TinkerMyHome** so you don't have to walk into all the dead ends I did. Everything here actually runs at my place – no theory from a vendor's catalogue.

## What you'll find here

- **Start here** – Home Assistant from zero, for complete beginners.
- **Energy & solar** – getting the most out of solar panels and a battery.
- **My home** – a tour of what I have and how it works.
- **DIY & ESP32, AI, reviews** – for those who want to go further.
- **Projects** – my open-source integrations and cards for Home Assistant, free.

## How this site is made

The site is built and co-written by AI (Claude) from my notes, configs and experience. I read and approve every article before it goes live.
